Hello, World!  

You are reading my first blog! I began it today, as of 17, Aug 2016, just a few days after I started my PhD in Mathematics.  

There has been such a great supply of math blogs, from research to pedagogy, and most of them are incredible. But why start another one (in the very likely case there is no audience)?  

Because it is (fun!) that lies in the effort to understand, in the process of explaining and possibly personalizing the narratives and making the material easier to penetrate. Here, it is possible to explain the chronology, the struggle, the doubts, etc. or *i.e*. the life of the work openly, with humour, with many languages, etc., where otherwise not possible.  

I said openly, yes, that means I will appreciate discussions that I hope will later happen to this blog project. I love your ideas and I am not in favor of keeping thoughts in the shadow.  

That said, one also takes the responsibility as any mature would (think of Trump?).  

Okay, let's start!
